# Rules for `src/apps/feed/components`

- NEMPO Bar Bumper (`NempoBarBumper`, tab "Bumper 4 · NEMPO Bar", 1080×1440, 8 s loop) plays one pointer-only MP4 (`nempo-rusa-bumper-kecil.mp4.asset.json`) that is also the download: AI image-to-video of the Sundanese golden-antlered white deer mascot (West Java style, never Chinese) walking around a glowing ring, opened and closed by gold velvet curtains under a gold valance, with the deer kept small in the lower half and a large, popping and floating NEMPO logo in the sky above it, composited by ffmpeg as a gold 3D bevel/extrusion made from the original logo alpha (shape never changed); why: the owner wanted a real-looking mascot and the logo's exact shape.
- NEMPO Bumper 5 (`NempoBumperTitle`, tab "Bumper 5 · Bajidoran DJ", 1080×1440, 8 s loop) is the gold "BAJIDORAN DJ" lettering alone — no deer, the owner says the deer already opens the feed — over the AI-cleaned night-stage plate: the lettering is an RGBA sprite (float, glow pulse, glint sweep masked by its own alpha) and the real curtain texture is split into two `scaleX` panels under a static valance. The component renders only for the `?nbt=css` recording pass, whose 240 stepped frames encode into `nempo-bumper-05-bajidoran-dj-anim.mp4`, the file the tab plays and downloads; recapture from that pass whenever the plate, sprite or curtain texture changes; why: the download must equal the preview and the lettering must stay exactly as filmed.
- NEMPO Bumper 6 (`NempoBumperLineup`, tab "Bumper 6 · Pengisi Acara", 1080×1440, 8 s loop) introduces the performers (MC, DJ Dityar, Kendang Jaipong, Penari Bajidor) as cards cropped from the slide 63 poster over its blurred warm backdrop with poster-style 3D lettering; rendered only for the `?nbl=css` pass, whose 240 stepped frames encode `nempo-bumper-06-pengisi-acara.mp4`, the file the tab plays and downloads; recapture after changes; why: the original performer cut-outs are locked in another project and download must equal preview.
